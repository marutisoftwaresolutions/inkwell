# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Inkwell's issues, discussions and pull requests to be a place where anyone can take part regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being clear, patient and specific, especially with newcomers.
- Accepting constructive criticism and giving it kindly.
- Focusing on what is best for users of the software.
- Assuming good faith and asking before judging.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and sexualised language or imagery.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of threads or reviews.

## Scope

This applies to every space managed by the project: the repository, issues, discussions, pull requests, and any public channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@useinkwell.app**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not follow this code and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban anyone who breaches it.
